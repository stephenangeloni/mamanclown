Creative Workshops

Fun and Educational Activities

Discover our creative and interactive workshops specially designed for children ages 2 to 8! Maman Clown offers fun activities that stimulate children's creativity and imagination.

Each workshop is a unique adventure where children learn while having fun, in a joyful and safe atmosphere.

Our Workshops

  • Face Painting Workshop - Magical transformations into princesses, superheroes, animals and more
  • Balloon Workshop - Colorful balloon sculptures: flowers, animals, funny hats
  • Magic Workshop - Introduction to simple and fun magic tricks
  • Creative Games Workshop - Manual activities, drawing and themed crafts
  • Musical Workshop - Songs, instruments and rhythmic games

Workshop Packages

Discovery Workshop (45 min) - One activity of your choice for a first experience
Complete Workshop (1h30) - Two combined workshops for more diversity
Creative Day (3h) - Full program with workshop rotation and snack time

Where and When?

Our workshops are available in Monaco, Monte-Carlo and the entire French Riviera. Perfect for schools, activity centers, private or public events.

Total flexibility: we adapt to your needs and schedule!
