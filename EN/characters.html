Maman Clown

Gentle, Joyful, Magical

Maman Clown is a gentle and colorful character who brings joy and wonder to children in Monaco and the French Riviera. With her rainbow colors and warm smile, she creates unforgettable magical moments.

  • Interactive magic tricks adapted for little ones
  • Creative balloon sculptures
  • Musical games and catchy songs
  • Artistic face painting and glitter
  • Captivating and participatory stories

Themed Entertainment

Customizable to your wishes

Maman Clown can adapt her show according to your event's theme: princesses, superheroes, animals, nature, or any other magical universe that will delight your child.

  • Costumes adaptable to themes
  • Personalized props and decorations
  • Custom entertainment
  • Surprises adapted to children's ages

Rainbow Show

Colors, Joy, Wonder

Maman Clown's signature show celebrates the colors of the rainbow through magic, music and active participation. Each child leaves with colorful memories and a bright smile.

  • Interactive rainbow-themed activities
  • Colorful balloon creations
  • Songs celebrating colors and diversity
  • Glitter and sparkle magic
